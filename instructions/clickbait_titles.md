# Clickbait Title Formulas

## Core Principles
- Every title must create a curiosity gap — hint at a payoff without giving it away.
- Include the primary keyword naturally within the first 5 words when possible.
- Target high CTR through specificity, numbers, emotion, or surprise.
- Avoid generic titles. Replace "Best Tips" with "7 Counterintuitive Tips That..."
- Titles must be honest — no misleading clickbait that the article can't deliver on.

## High-Performance Title Formulas

### Number + Adjective + Keyword + Promise
> "7 Hidden [Topic] Spots That Locals Never Share"
> "5 Surprising Things About [Topic] You Didn't Expect"

### Question Formula
> "Is [Topic] Really Worth It? The Honest Truth"
> "What Nobody Tells You About [Topic] Before You Go"

### Curiosity Gap + Keyword
> "The [Topic] Secret That Changed How We Travel"
> "Why [Topic] Is Not What You Think (And That's a Good Thing)"

### How-To + Specific Outcome
> "How to Experience [Topic] Like a Local (Without the Tourist Tax)"
> "How We Found the Best [Topic] With Zero Planning"

### Controversy / Counterintuitive
> "[Topic]: The Overrated Version vs. The Real Experience"
> "Stop Doing [Topic] This Way — Do This Instead"

### Listicle + Authority Signal
> "10 [Topic] Experiences That Will Make You Cancel Your Mainstream Plans"
> "The Only [Topic] Guide You'll Ever Need in [Year]"

## Requirements for Every Generated Title Set
- Generate exactly 10 titles per request.
- Cover at least 3 different formula types.
- Primary keyword must appear in at least 7 of the 10 titles.
- Mix question-based, number-based, and statement-based formats.
- At least 2 titles must feel urgent or time-sensitive.
- Tone must adapt to the assigned persona (formal expert vs. adventurous storyteller).

## Prohibited Patterns
- No "Ultimate Guide to..." as the first 3 words (overused, penalized in CTR).
- No ALL CAPS words (spammy signal).
- No titles over 70 characters (gets truncated in SERPs).
- No fabricated statistics in titles (e.g., "90% of people don't know...") unless verifiable.
